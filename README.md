# Honors Master List

A curated list of anime and manga for an ENGL C1000 honors project at San Diego Mesa College. Two curators choose what goes in and write the summaries; this repository is their working record.

- **The list:** `index.html` — filters by type, queer tag, genre, year, rating, format and streaming site; sort by rating, users, year or title.
- **The data:** `db.json` — 7,600 titles (every anime and manga with 10,000+ AniList users, adult titles excluded; queer-tagged titles down to 1,000), pulled from AniList on Oct 8, 2026. Scores, user counts, tags, blurbs and streaming links are AniList's.
- **The curation:** `marks.json` — one entry per title the curators have marked keep, cut or rewrite, with their reason and, for rewrites, their own summary. Every change is a commit by the person who made it; the git history is the record of who chose what, and when. The curators' 76 picks so far are marked keep, recorded as `by: "pride list"`.
- **Research notes:** `notes.json` — background research on 50 of the picks (the world, LGBTQ+ angle, general impact, sources), shown as a collapsible block on each card. It is reference material, kept separate from the curators' own "why it matters" lines.

Source data © AniList and the respective publishers. Curation © the curators.
